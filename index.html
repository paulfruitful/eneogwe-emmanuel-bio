ENEOGWE I.N. EMMANUEL

President — International Human Rights Protection Service (Nigeria)

Entrepreneur • Human Capacity Development Advocate • Youth Empowerment Champion

ENEOGWE I.N. EMMANUEL is a Nigerian-born entrepreneur, human capacity development advocate, champion of youth empowerment and self-actualisation, and a Kingdom-driven visionary with a distinctive divine apostolic calling and disposition.

Emmanuel Eneogwe is a focused, purpose-driven individual who believes in maintaining a positive mindset, pursuing excellence, doing what is right, and upholding values firmly anchored in Kingdom principles.

An active, pragmatic, resourceful and exceptionally meticulous personality, Emmanuel is a man whose life journey has been shaped by the diverse challenges, experiences and demanding realities of life. Having grown through the thorns and thickets of life's formative experiences, he developed a resilient mindset, a clear sense of direction and an unwavering determination to make meaningful contributions to humanity.

These experiences further strengthened his resolve to become a selfless enabler, mentor and pathway-builder, committed to preparing, supporting and providing opportunities for the next generation to discover their potential and advance into different spheres of human endeavour.

Armed with a degree in Business Administration, Emmanuel Eneogwe ventured into the construction industry with an expansive vision, a large heart and an extraordinary drive for enterprise, innovation and sustainable success.

Today, he serves on the boards of well over eleven (11) corporate entities with diverse interests spanning engineering and construction, mechanised agriculture and allied services, oil and gas exploration and development, ICT training and empowerment, media and communications, human capacity development, hydro-cleaning, environmental management and control, among other strategic sectors.

Corporate Entities Within His Portfolio Include:
  1. Bokans Engineering and Construction Ltd.
  2. Eastern Keys Ltd.
  3. Emmanetic Energy Ltd.
  4. Eastern Volume Ltd.
  5. The Truth Channel Inc.
  6. Eastern Technologies Ltd.
  7. And several other corporate interests.

Beyond business and corporate leadership, Emmanuel's overarching vision remains centred on enterprise development, human empowerment, purposeful leadership and the creation of sustainable pathways through which individuals, particularly young people, can rise, achieve and make meaningful contributions to society.

His philosophy is simple: to succeed with purpose, lead with integrity, serve with sacrifice and become a ladder through which others can rise.
